    How proof of work is measured

    Each organisation's logged projects are counted at five stages, earliest on the left. The line is filled up to the furthest stage they have reached. A yellow point means work is live on MainNet.

    Building
    In design, built locally, or running on TestNet.
    Delivered
    Demo or POC completed.
    Live
    Live on MainNet.

    Institution-ready

    Serves institutional clients and has work live or delivered. Start here when an institution asks for an SI.

    Client category

    Based on the clients and products we can see, not on what the firm says about itself. Each profile gives the reason. Institutional is reserved for partners with proven delivery for large financial institutions, ideally in production.

    Tier (shown on profiles)

    Core
    Super Validator, or Foundation member with MainNet work, or a regulated partner with live deployments.
    Active
    Verified Canton footprint with lighter production evidence.
    Early
    Some public Canton signal but no tracked project.
    Pipeline
    A target; no Canton activity found yet, or not researched.
